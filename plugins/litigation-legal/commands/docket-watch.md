Locate and brief the litigation matter described by:

**$ARGUMENTS**

Use the **litigation-tracking** skill:

1. Resolve to a specific docket (CourtListener RECAP for federal; Trellis for
   state). If several match, list the top candidates with court, filing date,
   and parties, and confirm which one before going deep.
2. Map the docket: caption, court, parties and counsel, cause(s) of action,
   posture, assigned judge. Pull the operative complaint and most recent
   substantive filings, reading the key ones rather than summarizing the docket
   text alone.
3. Produce a docket brief with links to the key filings, and note any access
   limits (sealed entries, paywalls, coverage gaps).
4. **Offer** to set up a docket alert and/or a standing search alert, stating
   exactly what will trigger a notification. **Confirm with me before creating
   or deleting any alert** — do not subscribe automatically.

End with the disclaimer that docket data may lag and can be incomplete, and is
research support only — not legal advice.
